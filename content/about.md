I'm a software engineer from Belgium with a passion for learning and sharing what I discover.

Writing is one of the ways I learn. Many of the posts on this blog start with a question I had myself. As I research the answer, I write it down in the way I would have liked it to be explained when I was learning.

I also use this blog to share the tools, technologies, and workflows that I enjoy using, in the hope that others might find them useful too.
